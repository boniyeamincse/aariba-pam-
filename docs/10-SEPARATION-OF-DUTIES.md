# 10 · Separation of Duties: Ariba PAM

> Conflict Prevention, Four-Eyes Principle & Controlled Exceptions · 2026-11-22

Separation of Duties (SoD) is a mandatory architectural control in Ariba PAM. Its purpose is to prevent fraud, unauthorized privilege escalation, and unilateral compromise by ensuring that critical operations require at least two distinct individuals.

---

## 1. Core Separation of Duties Rules

Ariba PAM defines and enforces five non-negotiable SoD rules across the control plane:

| Rule ID | Conflicting Roles / Actions | Enforcement Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SOD-01** | **Self-Approval Prohibition**: Requester cannot approve their own access request. | Control plane checks `req.requester_id == current_user.id` and rejects with HTTP 403 `SELF_APPROVAL_FORBIDDEN`. Also prevents approval via delegation. |
| **SOD-02** | **Independent Policy Review**: Asset owner or author cannot approve their own policy proposal. | Admin change proposal checks `proposal.author_id == current_user.id` and rejects with HTTP 403 `SELF_REVIEW_DENIED`. |
| **SOD-03** | **Privileged Role Self-Assignment**: PAM Administrators cannot grant themselves or extend their own privileged roles. | Role assignment endpoint validates `target_user_id != caller_user_id` for administrative roles. |
| **SOD-04** | **Emergency Access Independence**: Emergency Custodians cannot independently activate break-glass credentials. | Multi-party threshold scheme requires quorum of distinct trustees. |
| **SOD-05** | **Evidence Custody Independence**: Evidence Custodians cannot alter, truncate, or delete evidence generated by their own sessions or operations. | Dedicated WORM storage policies block write and delete operations; audit trails record independent SHA-256 hashes. |

---

## 2. Real-Time SoD Evaluation

When a user submits an access request, records an approval, or requests a role assignment:
1. The engine scans active `sod_rules` for conflicting permissions.
2. If a rule match occurs:
   - **Strict Mode**: The transaction is aborted with HTTP 403 / 409 `SOD_CONFLICT`.
   - An entry is logged in `sod_violations` recording the attempted action, offending user, and timestamp.
   - An alert event is dispatched via the audit outbox to Security Operations.

---

## 3. Controlled Exception Workflow

In urgent operational circumstances (e.g. disaster recovery, lean teams on night shift), an organization may permit a temporary SoD waiver through a strictly governed exception workflow:

```
[ Engineer Requests SoD Exception ]
             │
             ├── Provides: Rule ID, Scope, Justification, Required Duration (max 8h)
             ▼
[ Dual Approval Required ]
             ├── 1. Security Operations Lead Review
             └── 2. Compliance / Risk Officer Review
             ▼
[ Temporary SoD Exception Issued ]
             ├── Time-bound entry created in `sod_exceptions`
             ├── High-priority SOC notification emitted
             └── Every action performed under exception is flagged with `[SOD_WAIVER]`
             ▼
[ Automatic Expiry or Operator Revocation ]
```

- **Maximum Duration**: 8 hours. Exceptions cannot be renewed automatically.
- **Audit Requirement**: The full transcript of all sessions conducted under an active SoD exception is automatically flagged for mandatory next-business-day compliance review.
